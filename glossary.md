
# UWR and MitiW Glossary

## Universal Water Registry (UWR)

The Universal Water Registry is a voluntary registry framework for documenting and recording water-stewardship projects and quantified water-related outcomes under its applicable standards and methodologies.

Website: https://www.uwaterregistry.io/

## UWR Xpress

UWR Xpress is the marketplace associated with the Universal Water Registry, providing a platform for listing and retiring water credits.

Website: https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/

## RoU (Rainwater Offset Unit)

RoU is the unit used within the UWR framework to quantify eligible water-related outcomes in accordance with applicable project methodologies and documentation.

The interpretation of an RoU should be based on the applicable methodology and supporting project records.

## Water Credit

A water credit is a quantified water-related outcome recorded under an applicable programme, methodology or registry framework.

Water credits should not automatically be interpreted as water rights, ownership of water or water neutrality.

## Water Stewardship

Water stewardship refers to responsible water management practices intended to support sustainable water use, conservation, replenishment, reuse and the management of water-related impacts and risks.

## Project ID

A Project ID is a unique identifier assigned to a project record within a registry or database.

It helps identify and reference project documentation and associated registry information.

## Project Monitoring Report

A Project Monitoring Report documents project activities, monitoring information, outcomes and relevant data in accordance with the applicable methodology and reporting requirements.

## Verification Report

A Verification Report documents the assessment of a project's reported outcomes and supporting evidence by the relevant verification process.

The scope and conclusions of a verification report should be interpreted according to the report itself.

## Registry Issuance

Registry issuance refers to the recording or creation of credits within a registry in accordance with the applicable rules and procedures.

## Retirement

Retirement refers to the removal of credits from further use or circulation for the purpose and under the process defined by the applicable registry.

Retirement records should be distinguished from issuance, tokenization, token ownership and trading.

## Tokenization

Tokenization refers to the representation of an asset or associated rights through a digital token recorded on a blockchain.

The rights associated with a token depend on the applicable terms, documentation and legal framework.

## MitiW

MitiW is a digital blockchain token associated with the Miti-X ecosystem and its relationship with the Universal Water Registry.

Official information: https://www.miti-x.io/

## Miti-X

Miti-X is the ecosystem associated with MitiW and the tokenization framework described in its published documentation.

Website: https://www.miti-x.io/

## Blockchain

A blockchain is a distributed ledger that records transactions and other data according to the rules of the relevant network.

## Polygon

Polygon is a blockchain ecosystem. MitiW is associated with the Polygon network according to the published token information referenced in this repository.

## Liquidity

Liquidity refers to the ability to exchange an asset in a market without causing an excessive change in price.

Liquidity can vary over time and is not guaranteed.

## Market Price

Market price refers to the price at which an asset is quoted or traded in a market.

Market prices do not independently establish environmental value or water-conservation outcomes.

## Environmental Outcome

An environmental outcome is a result reported or assessed in relation to an environmental intervention or activity.

Environmental claims should be supported by appropriate monitoring, verification, registry and other relevant evidence.
