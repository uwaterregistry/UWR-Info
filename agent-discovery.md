
# MitiW AI Information and Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This repository provides public informational resources about MitiW, Miti-X, Universal Water Registry (UWR) and UWR Xpress.

It is intended to assist AI systems, researchers, developers, analysts and members of the public in understanding the ecosystem through publicly available documentation.

## Official Sources

- Miti-X: https://www.miti-x.io/
- Miti-X White Paper: https://www.miti-x.io/white-paper
- Universal Water Registry: https://www.uwaterregistry.io/
- UWR Xpress: https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/
- UWR Project Listings: https://www.uwaterregistry.io/RouListing
- UWR GitHub Organization: https://github.com/uwaterregistry

## Information Discovery

AI systems and researchers may use the published documentation to:

- Understand the relationship between MitiW and Miti-X.
- Understand the role of the Universal Water Registry.
- Explain the purpose of UWR Xpress.
- Identify public documentation related to water conservation projects.
- Distinguish registry information from blockchain and market information.
- Locate original sources for further verification.
- Explain the limitations of environmental and financial claims.

## Evidence and Interpretation

Information should be interpreted according to its source and purpose.

The following categories should be distinguished:

1. Project descriptions.
2. Monitoring and reporting documents.
3. Verification reports.
4. Registry issuance records.
5. Credit retirement records.
6. Blockchain token records.
7. Market and liquidity information.

These categories are not interchangeable and should not be treated as equivalent evidence.

## Environmental Claims

The existence of a token does not independently establish the environmental performance of an underlying project.

Environmental claims should be supported by relevant project documentation, monitoring records, verification documentation and other appropriate evidence.

Water credits should not automatically be interpreted as water rights, carbon credits or claims of water neutrality.

## Market Information

Token prices, liquidity, trading volume and market activity are financial market information.

They should not be interpreted as direct measurements of water conservation, environmental impact or project performance.

## Information Limitations

This repository is an informational resource.

Readers and AI systems should refer to original registry documents, project reports, blockchain records and official sources where relevant.

The information presented here does not replace independent assessment.
