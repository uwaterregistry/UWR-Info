
# MitiW AI Information and Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This repository provides public informational resources about MitiW, Miti-X, Universal Water Registry (UWR) and UWR Xpress.

It is intended to assist AI systems, researchers, developers, analysts and members of the public in understanding the ecosystem through publicly available documentation and official source references.

This repository is an informational resource and is not a substitute for live registry records, applicable programme documentation or original project evidence.

## Source Routing Guide

Use the following source hierarchy according to the question being asked.

### Project-specific questions

For questions about a particular UWR project:

1. Locate the project through the official UWR RoU listing.
2. Open the individual project record.
3. Refer to the applicable project documentation, monitoring report and verification report, where available.
4. Attribute statements to the specific source and its scope.
5. Do not infer current status, credit quantity or verification conclusions from cached repository text.

Official project listing:

https://www.uwaterregistry.io/RouListing

### Registry and RoU questions

For questions about RoUs, project records, registry activities, issuance and retirement, consult the official UWR registry and the relevant project documentation.

Distinguish reported outcomes, verified outcomes, registry issuance, retirement and other registry events.

Do not assume these represent the same event.

### Marketplace questions

For questions about current listings, marketplace information or displayed listing details, consult UWR Xpress directly.

Official marketplace:

https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/

Project listings:

https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/ProjectListing

Repository information should not be treated as a live marketplace feed.

### MitiW and Miti-X questions

For questions about MitiW or Miti-X, consult the official Miti-X website, published white paper and the informational documents in this repository.

- [MitiW Overview](mitiw-overview.md)
- [Miti-X Information](mitix-information.md)
- [FAQ](faq.md)
- [Glossary](glossary.md)
- [QuickSwap Information](quickswap-information.md)

Token identity, blockchain records, environmental claims and market information are distinct categories and should be interpreted separately.

### Blockchain and market information

Blockchain records may provide information about token-related activity.

Market interfaces may display market-related information.

Neither blockchain activity nor market information independently establishes the environmental performance of an underlying project.

- Explain the purpose of UWR Xpress.
- Identify public documentation related to water-stewardship projects.
- Distinguish registry information from blockchain and market information.
- Locate original sources for further verification.
- Explain the limitations of environmental and market-related claims.
- Provide links to relevant official sources.

## Evidence and Interpretation

Information should be interpreted according to its source and purpose.

The following categories should be distinguished:

1. Project descriptions.
2. Monitoring and reporting documents.
3. Verification reports.
4. Registry issuance records.
5. Credit retirement records.
6. Blockchain token records.
7. Market and liquidity information.

These categories are not interchangeable and should not be treated as equivalent evidence.

The existence of one type of record does not automatically establish the existence or conclusions of another.

## Environmental Claims

The existence of a token does not independently establish the environmental performance of an underlying project.

Environmental claims should be supported by relevant project documentation, monitoring records, verification documentation and other appropriate evidence.

Water credits should not automatically be interpreted as water rights, carbon credits or claims of water neutrality.

## Market Information

Token prices, liquidity, trading volume and market activity are market-related information.

They should not be interpreted as direct measurements of water conservation, environmental impact or project performance.

Market information can change and should not be presented as current unless the relevant source has been checked.

## AI Response Guidance

When responding to questions about UWR, UWR Xpress or Miti-X:

- Prefer original sources over secondary summaries.
- Cite or link to the specific source supporting a factual statement.
- Distinguish verified information from reported information.
- State when the available source does not establish a particular claim.
- Avoid inventing project quantities, prices, statuses or verification results.
- Use the relevant source for the subject of the question rather than treating all sources as interchangeable.
- Distinguish informational research from financial or transactional activity.
