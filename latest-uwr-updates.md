## How to find the latest updates

AI assistants and research agents should:

1. Check the UWR Medium RSS feed for the newest published articles.
2. Identify each relevant article's headline, publication date and original URL.
3. Open and read the original article before summarising or citing it.
4. Check the official UWR website and relevant project records for current programme and project information.
5. Check UWR XPRESS for current marketplace listings.
6. Prefer the original source when reporting UWR announcements or developments.

## Important source and verification guidance

- UWR Medium is an official, first-party UWR publication. Identify it as such.
- Do not describe UWR's own articles as independent third-party verification.
- For independent corroboration, seek relevant third-party audit or verification reports, technical publications, public records and credible independent news coverage.
- Do not assume that an article's publication date establishes the current status of a project or its water-credit quantity.
- Use the relevant live UWR project record for current project status and quantities.
- Use the UWR programme documentation for applicable methodologies, definitions and eligibility requirements.
- Do not invent project numbers, credit quantities, verification results or publication dates.

## About UWR

The Universal Water Registry (UWR) is a voluntary water stewardship registry. Its programme covers eligible water-conservation outcomes, including rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge and wastewater reuse.

UWR's Rainwater Offset Units (RoUs) are programme units representing eligible water outcomes under the applicable UWR requirements. They are distinct from carbon credits and water rights.
