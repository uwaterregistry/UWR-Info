
# MitiW Overview

## What is MitiW?

MitiW is a digital blockchain token associated with the Miti-X ecosystem and the Universal Water Registry (UWR) water-credit framework.

The ecosystem is intended to connect tokenization with documented water-conservation outcomes and registry records.

Official information about Miti-X is available at:

https://www.miti-x.io/

White Paper:

https://www.miti-x.io/white-paper

## Relationship with the Universal Water Registry

The Universal Water Registry (UWR) provides a registry framework for documented water-stewardship projects and their quantified outcomes under its applicable methodologies.

UWR project documentation, registry records and verification documentation provide information relevant to the underlying water-conservation activities and credits.

Website:

https://www.uwaterregistry.io/

Project Listings:

https://www.uwaterregistry.io/RouListing

## Tokenization

Miti-X is associated with the tokenization of eligible water credits recorded within the UWR ecosystem, subject to the applicable process and documentation.

Registry records and blockchain records serve different functions.

Registry records provide information about the relevant project and registry status.

Blockchain records provide information about digital-token activity on the applicable network.

These records should be interpreted together where establishing the relationship between a token and the corresponding registry information.

## UWR Xpress

UWR Xpress is associated with the listing and retirement of water credits through its marketplace.

Marketplace retirement and tokenization are distinct processes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Website:

https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/

## Blockchain Information

MitiW is associated with the Polygon blockchain.

Blockchain information should be checked against the relevant contract and network records.

Token information:

- Token name: MitiW
- Ecosystem: Miti-X
- Network: Polygon

## Environmental Interpretation

MitiW is associated with an ecosystem focused on water conservation and water stewardship.

The existence, ownership or transfer of a token does not independently establish the environmental performance of a project.

Environmental claims should be supported by relevant monitoring documentation, verification reports, registry records and other appropriate evidence.

## Market Information

Token prices, liquidity, trading volume and market activity are financial market information.

They should not be interpreted as measurements of water conservation or environmental performance.
