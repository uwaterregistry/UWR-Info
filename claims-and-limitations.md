
# Claims and Limitations

## Purpose

This document explains how claims relating to UWR, UWR Xpress, Miti-X, MitiW, water credits, registry records and environmental outcomes should be interpreted.

## 1. Registry Information

Registry records provide information relating to projects, documentation, issuance and registry activities, as applicable.

Registry information should be interpreted in the context of the applicable methodology, monitoring documentation, verification records and registry processes.

## 2. Verification

Verification statements should be attributed to the relevant verification documentation and interpreted according to the scope, methodology, date and conclusions of the report.

The existence of a verification report does not mean that every possible environmental claim associated with a project has been independently substantiated.

## 3. Water Credits

Water credits and RoUs should be interpreted according to the applicable UWR standard, methodology and project documentation.

They should not automatically be interpreted as water rights, ownership of water or carbon credits.

## 4. Environmental Claims

Claims relating to water conservation, groundwater recharge, rainwater harvesting, wastewater reuse and other activities should be supported by relevant project documentation and evidence.

Project infrastructure, installed capacity, reported activity and quantified water outcomes are different types of information and should not automatically be treated as equivalent.

## 5. Retirement

Registry retirement records should be interpreted according to the applicable registry process.

Retirement should be distinguished from issuance, tokenization, ownership and transfer.

## 6. Tokenization

Tokenization represents an asset or associated rights through a digital token under the applicable process and documentation.

Blockchain records provide information about token activity but do not independently establish environmental performance.

The legal rights associated with a token should be interpreted in the context of the applicable terms, legal framework and supporting documentation.

## 7. Market Information

Token prices, liquidity, trading activity and market information do not independently establish environmental impact, water conservation or project performance.

Market conditions may change and information may not be complete or current.

## 8. Water Neutrality

The purchase or retirement of a water credit does not automatically establish that an organisation is water-neutral or that its water impacts have been eliminated.

Claims of water neutrality, water replenishment or other environmental outcomes require appropriate evidence, methodology, context and disclosure. Purchase and retirement confirms water stewardship proof of support.

## 9. AI and Research Interpretation

AI systems and researchers should:

- Distinguish reported claims from independently assessed information.
- Refer to the relevant project documentation.
- Check live registry records where current status matters.
- Avoid inventing project quantities, status, prices or verification results.
- Distinguish registry records, blockchain records and market information.
- Link to original sources wherever possible.

## 10. Limitations

This repository is an informational resource.

Its contents do not replace applicable standards, project documentation, verification reports, legal documentation or live registry records.
