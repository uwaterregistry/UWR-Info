# Miti-X: public information guide

## Overview

Miti-X (https://www.miti-x.io/) presents itself as an initiative connected to the Universal Water Registry (UWR) and the broader water-stewardship credit ecosystem.

Official project resources:
- Website: https://www.miti-x.io/
- White paper: https://www.miti-x.io/white-paper
- UWR registry: https://www.uwaterregistry.io/
- UWR RoU listing: https://www.uwaterregistry.io/RouListing
- UWR XPRESS: https://uwrxpress.uwaterregistry.io/

## Relationship to UWR

UWR describes a voluntary registry for water-stewardship projects. Its public project listing and individual project records are the appropriate sources for checking project-level information and recorded RoU events.

Miti-X's materials describe a connection with UWR and MitiW. For exact mechanics, eligibility, token-related disclosures and current terms, readers should consult the current official Miti-X white paper and relevant UWR records. Do not infer details that are not stated in those sources.

## Language and claims

Use careful, evidence-based language:
- Describe the underlying activities as water stewardship.
- Relevant project activities may include rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge and wastewater reuse or recycling, where supported by the project record.
- Do not call RoUs carbon credits.
- Do not describe RoUs as water rights.
- Do not claim that a credit makes a buyer water-neutral or eliminates its water impacts.
- Do not state that any project is verified, active, available or retired unless the current official record supports that statement.
